PinCommerce — curated lifestyle pins for UK audiences.

A UK Pinterest content network publishing editorially-curated pins across home, kitchen, garden, gifts and travel. Every pin links via AWIN deep tracking with full FTC affiliate disclosure on every post.

What we do

PinCommerce operates a small portfolio of Pinterest Business accounts, each focused on a tightly defined UK lifestyle audience. Pins are produced by an editorial pipeline that combines AI-generated copy, brand-curated product photography, and Pinterest-format scene generation. Every link is an AWIN deep-tracked URL; every description carries #ad and #affiliate tags in line with UK CAP Code and ASA guidance.

We are not a coupon site, a deal aggregator, or an SEO incentive scheme. We publish to engaged Pinterest audiences who use the platform for inspiration and considered purchase.

How we curate

  • Product feeds are pulled from AWIN's product feed API where available. We work directly from advertiser-published metadata — current price, in-stock status, brand-supplied imagery.
  • Editorial selection via Claude Sonnet ranking against board-specific themes and price bands. We don't post everything in a feed — we select.
  • Lifestyle composition via Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, which preserves the merchant's product image and places it in a scene appropriate to the board's audience.
  • Disclosure is non-negotiable: #ad #affiliate appears at the end of every pin description, with descriptive alt text and topic tags.
  • Rate limits are well under Pinterest's spam thresholds — accounts post on a distributed schedule across morning, lunch, and evening windows.

Brand standards

What we will not do:

  • Pin products without first-party imagery from the merchant or AWIN's product feed
  • Misrepresent prices, stock status, or product features
  • Run pins that lack the #ad disclosure or that hide the affiliate relationship
  • Pin products from sectors that conflict with brand safety (gambling, adult, alcohol, tobacco, weapons)
  • Use Pinterest's promotional ad units alongside affiliate pins (organic only)

What we always do:

  • Track every click through the merchant's AWIN-assigned deep link
  • Honour the merchant's brand guidelines on logo use, claims, and pricing
  • Pull tracking transparency status weekly and pause accounts where tracking has degraded
  • Respond to merchant requests to remove or adjust pins within 48 hours
