What we do

PinCommerce operates a small portfolio of Pinterest Business accounts, each focused on a tightly defined UK lifestyle audience. Pins combine AI-curated editorial copy, brand-supplied product imagery, and Pinterest-format scene generation. Every link is an AWIN deep-tracked URL; every description carries #ad and #affiliate tags in line with UK CAP Code and ASA guidance.

We are not a coupon site, deal aggregator, or SEO incentive scheme. We publish to engaged Pinterest audiences who use the platform for inspiration and considered purchase.

How we curate

  • Product feeds pulled from AWIN's product feed API where available. We work directly from advertiser-published metadata — current price, in-stock status, brand-supplied imagery.
  • Editorial selection via Claude Sonnet ranking against board-specific themes and price bands. We don't post everything in a feed — we select.
  • Lifestyle composition via Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, which preserves the merchant's product image and places it in a scene appropriate to the board's audience.
  • Disclosure is non-negotiable: #ad #affiliate on every pin, descriptive alt text, topic tags.
  • Rate limits well under Pinterest's spam thresholds — distributed posting across morning, lunch and evening windows.

Brand standards

Standards aren't aspirational here — they're enforced in code on every pin.

What we will not do

  • Pin products without first-party imagery from the merchant or AWIN's feed
  • Misrepresent prices, stock status, or product features
  • Run pins that lack #ad disclosure
  • Promote sectors that conflict with brand safety (gambling, adult, alcohol, tobacco, weapons)
  • Use Pinterest promotional ad units alongside affiliate pins (organic only)

What we always do

  • Track every click through the merchant's AWIN deep link
  • Honour the merchant's brand guidelines on logo use, claims, pricing
  • Pull tracking transparency status weekly; pause accounts where tracking has degraded
  • Respond to merchant requests to remove or adjust pins within 48 hours
  • Disclose AI-assisted content production where editorial reviewers ask
